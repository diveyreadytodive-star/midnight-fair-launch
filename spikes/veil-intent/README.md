# VeilIntent one-to-one payment escrow spike

VeilIntent is a simulator-first Compact prototype for one buyer, one approved seller, and one per-intent spending mandate. It checks a seller's public payment quote against the buyer's private maximum unit price and total budget, then allows the approved seller to claim a one-way payment from shielded escrow. It is **not an atomic asset swap**: no seller asset is escrowed or delivered.

Simulator fixtures use a public `150`-unit valueless payment-token lot, sample private max budget `102`, sample private max unit price `2.00`, public quantity `50`, and public seller quote `2.00` per item (`100` total). Those sample values are test fixtures in the public source, not a privacy claim. The guarded acceptance runner instead randomizes the exact max budget in `[101, 120]` and unit-price cap above the public quote, persisting them only in its ignored `0600` recovery manifest. The seller receives `100`; the buyer separately claims the `50`-unit remainder. The escrow lot is intentionally larger than the maximum budget range so the public deposit does not equal the exact max.

## Roles and proof boundary

- **Buyer** keeps the wallet seed, selects the per-intent limits, approves one seller identity and payout key, funds the fixed test lot, and owns the committed refund recipient. The buyer can cancel an open intent before the agent approves the quote.
- **Agent** receives only the policy witness and a limited `agentSecret`, never the buyer wallet seed. It proves that the public quote satisfies this one intent. The proof is witness-authorized; the transaction submitter is not cryptographically bound to the agent.
- **Seller** has a precommitted identity and recipient. Its `sellerSecret` authenticates `submitQuote`; after agent approval, `sellerClaimPayout` sends to that same committed recipient. The later Local Devnet acceptance must use the seller's wallet/provider and verify that the seller actually discovers the received shielded coin.
- **Solver/relayer** sees the seller's public quote and may relay a proof produced by the authorized agent, but cannot produce the private policy proof without the agent witness and cannot change either committed recipient.

The contract has seven exported proof circuits: test mint, create intent, submit quote, approve quote, seller payout claim, buyer remainder claim, and buyer cancellation. Quote submission and agent approval do not transfer funds. The seller's own claim sends the payment and stores any escrow change for the buyer. Only one seller quote can be submitted per intent. If the agent rejects it, the buyer must cancel and create a fresh intent to try another quote; the demo must not pretend a second quote can be submitted on the same intent. Deadline checks apply to quote submission and approval; once approved, the seller may claim after the deadline so a slow settlement does not strand an approved payment. The buyer cannot cancel after approval, so seller non-cooperation or loss of its claim secret can still lock the test escrow. This is a deliberate one-intent prototype limitation, not a production liveness guarantee.

## Privacy and scope limits

The public ledger exposes the `150`-unit escrow lot, deadline, approved-seller commitments, actual quote quantity, actual unit price, actual quote total, and settlement state. That quote and the lot reveal a useful range for the private max budget; this is not full order privacy. The simulator-level field check verifies only that max-price/max-budget terms, nonce, salt, and role secrets are absent from the generated public ledger object. It is not a raw transaction, timing, recipient, or side-channel privacy audit.

This prototype has one intent per contract. Its nonce and status prevent replay of that intent; there is no cross-intent nonce registry, daily-spend limit, reusable mandate, multi-solver auction, orderbook, token swap, or production seller delivery guarantee. The payment token is valueless and locally minted for tests. A successful simulator claim is not evidence of Local Devnet settlement.

## Run

From this directory, run `npm test`. It compiles Compact 0.31.1, strict-typechecks the simulator and acceptance runner, and checks private price and budget rejection, quote-quantity mismatch, malicious division witnesses, seller and agent authorization, commitment/nonce tampering, expiry, buyer cancellation, claim-recipient binding, replay, and the successful `150 → 100 seller + 50 buyer remainder` flow. The simulator's public-field check only inspects the in-memory ledger object; it is not a raw transaction privacy audit.

The guarded Local Devnet runner is `scripts/test-local-chain.ts`; it has `--prepare-only`, `--preflight-only`, and `--execute` modes. Invoke it by its spike path from `private-perps/` (for example, `node --import tsx spikes/veil-intent/scripts/test-local-chain.ts --prepare-only`); do not use the repository's root `test:chain` script. Recovery is isolated at `private-perps/.local/veil-intent-recovery` (0700 directory, 0600 manifest), with no reuse of any SILENCE recovery bundle. An exclusive 0600 lock prevents concurrent runs and remains in place after interruption; live or stale locks fail closed and are never removed automatically. The one-process fixture holds buyer, seller, and solver test seeds, and the buyer genesis wallet sponsors DUST fees; this does not establish independently operated buyer, agent, and solver services. The runner is loopback-only and does not start or reset Docker. It refuses execution unless the manifest is freshly preflighted with no prior action, pending operation, or contract address. On any uncertain proof/transaction failure it preserves the pending marker and has no automatic resume path.

### Local Devnet acceptance, 2026-09-26

The complete test-token flow succeeded in blocks `4222–4246`: contract deploy, mint of the public `150`-unit lot, intent creation, authenticated seller quote, private agent approval, seller-wallet claim of `100`, and buyer-wallet claim of `50` change. The runner read back the quote and final contract state; the repository lead independently queried all seven receipts and reopened the buyer and seller wallets in a separate process to confirm balances of `50` and `100`. The change coin had ledger `mt_index=103` before the buyer claimed it. Sanitized receipt, hash, block, quote, and ledger evidence is in [`local-devnet-veil-intent.json`](docs/evidence/local-devnet-veil-intent.json).

This verifies one-way shielded payment from a valueless Local Devnet test token. It does not exchange or deliver a seller asset, establish production privacy, or prove independently operated agent/solver services. Actual quote and escrow values are public; the exact randomized limits were retained only in the protected recovery bundle and omitted from public evidence. The in-memory ledger field check is not a raw transaction privacy audit.
